The EP

Five tracks, one long exhale.

Zerenity moves from stillness to stillness. It opens with a warm, cosmic track built for settling in, calming keys ideal for the first few minutes of practice. From there it drifts into a piece with a subtle pulsing undercurrent, giving the record its heartbeat, before easing into a track with light drums and electric guitar — a groove to move through, not dance to.

The fourth movement lifts, uplifting and open. The EP closes with a reprise, cosmic string movements returning to bring the body and mind back to rest. A simple arrangement throughout: strings, guitar, light percussion. Nothing asking for your attention — everything there to hold the room.

“It does what it says on the tin.” Huw, Photographer

For yoga & wellness teachers

Music that holds the room, not the spotlight.

Zerenity was written to sit quietly underneath a class — no vocals, no sudden shifts, nothing to pull focus from breath or movement. It's used today by teachers running beginners, restorative, and relaxation-focused sessions.

Try it in a class, completely free, no obligation. If it's useful, there's room to go further — credit, joint promotion, or a limited-run physical CD your students can take home.
